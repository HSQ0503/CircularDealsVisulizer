Dear Professor Yan,

Thank you for the detailed feedback. Quick replies to each point.

**On private firm coverage.** That makes sense. I will keep news and press releases as the primary source for private side flows, with explicit reliability and confidence scoring at the per-source level so the selection bias stays transparent in the final dataset.

**On cost basis vs. fair value.** My Loop Score has a Balance Ratio component that measures flow symmetry between two companies. Cost basis ($13B for Microsoft's OpenAI investment) creates an asymmetric edge against OpenAI's much larger cloud commitment, while fair value ($135B) reflects current economic exposure and produces a more symmetric edge. Given your point that finance research generally prefers market value, I am inclined to use fair value and document the choice clearly. Does that match what you would recommend?

**On the sampling universe.** My current starting point is purposive (prominent AI companies and infrastructure providers), which is the bias I am trying to fix. I am planning to define the new universe as the union of companies appearing in the CB Insights AI 100, the Stanford AI Index reports, and the Forbes AI 50 over 2022 to 2025. That gives me a defined, rule-based set of companies to scope the data collection around.

**On the 451 Research dataset.** Yes please, I would be very grateful if you could try to get access. Ideally I could filter it to just the companies in my sampling frame. The owner, provider, and client structure would give me infrastructure relationships as structured data, which is the hardest layer to find through press releases. Combined with EDGAR and news for equity and hardware deals, it would let me cover all the flow types in my paper with much better quality than I currently have.

In the meantime, I will start building the sampling frame and updating data quality on the existing dataset, so I am not blocked while you check on access.

Happy to schedule a follow up call whenever works for you.

Thank you again,
